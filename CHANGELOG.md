## v2.3 - THE IMPROVEMENT UPDATE
- **NEW**: AIM DOWN SIGHTS option: choose hold or toggle in Settings.
- **NEW**: Low health heartbeat: it speeds up the closer you are to dying.
- **NEW**: Low ammo warning: your ammo counter turns red and pulses when the magazine is nearly empty.
- **NEW**: Tips on the main menu: a new hint every time you open it.

- **NEW**: Floating damage numbers pop up where you hit an enemy (gold and bigger for headshots). Toggle in Settings.

- **NEW**: Reload bar: a thin bar under your ammo counter shows how far the reload is.
- **NEW**: Low health warning: your health bar pulses when you are in trouble.
- **NEW**: ADS SENSITIVITY slider: tune your look speed while aiming separately.
- **NEW**: SCREEN SHAKE slider: turn camera shake down or off.
- **NEW**: SHOW FPS toggle in Settings.
- **NEW**: Phone vibration: a little buzz when you get hurt or score a kill (can be turned off).
- **NEW**: Soft click sounds on menu buttons.
- **NEW**: Match results now show your accuracy, kills, headshots and deaths.
- **IMPROVED**: Pause screen shows your kills and the time left in the match.
- **IMPROVED**: The game pauses itself and mutes when you switch apps or tabs, then picks the sound back up when you return.
